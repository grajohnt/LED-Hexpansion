# LED Filament Hexpansion for Tildagon Badge

A very simple hexpansion that takes the 3.3V from the Tildagon badge and adds a resistor and a pot for current-controlled dimming. This allows you to control the brightness of your LED and save battery life on the badge. 


![](Filament_Hexpansions.jpg)

## Usage

There are two large and ornate pads that can accomodate a wide variety of LEDs and LED filaments on both sides of the badge. This means that regardless of the polarity of your filament, you can add it to the front or back and have it be the right way around.



## Components

The pot is a BOURNS 3362P-1-101LF 100 ohm pot, and the resistor is really any old choose-your-wattage 10 ohm resistor. These can of course be varied in value, but this seems to provide a good variety of brightnesses.
